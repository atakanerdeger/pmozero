Privacy Policy

PMOZero — Effective April 14, 2025

1. Overview

PMOZero ("we", "our", "the app") is a health and recovery application designed to help users overcome pornography addiction. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: Email address and user ID (via Apple Sign In or email/password authentication through Supabase).
  • App usage data: Streak start date, completed tasks, journal entries, trigger counts, relapse dates, and custom activities. All stored locally on your device (AsyncStorage).
  • Purchase data: Subscription status managed by RevenueCat. We do not store payment card information.
  • AI analysis: When you use the AI coach feature, the selected trigger category and streak count are sent to our secure server (Supabase Edge Functions) to generate a response. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account and keep you logged in.
  • To provide personalised recovery features (streak tracking, AI coaching, daily quotes).
  • To manage your subscription status.
  • To send you opt-in daily motivation notifications.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and serverless functions (supabase.com)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (revenuecat.com)
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — AI coaching responses, accessed via our server only
  • Apple — Sign In with Apple, App Store payments, push notifications

Each third party has its own privacy policy and data practices.

5. Data Storage & Security

App usage data is stored locally on your device. Account and subscription data is stored on Supabase's secure, encrypted servers. We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your data within the app.
  • Deletion: You can delete all local data via the "Reset All Data" option in the app's Profile screen. To delete your account and server-side data, contact us at the email below.
  • Opt-out: You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

7. Children

PMOZero is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 17.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be communicated within the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
